IELTS Writing Task 2-এ linker / discourse marker ঠিকমতো ব্যবহার করলে ideaগুলো পরিষ্কার হয় — Band 6 থেকে 7-এ উঠতে এটা অন্যতম practical উপায়। নিচে 50টি useful linker আছে বাংলা অর্থ ও উদাহরণসহ (unofficial practice)।

Band tip
একই linker বারবার ব্যবহার করবেন না। Addition-এর জন্য moreover, contrast-এর জন্য however / on the other hand, result-এর জন্য therefore / as a result — এভাবে mix করুন। Forced academic words দিয়ে sentence ভাঙবেন না।

কেন linker গুরুত্বপূর্ণ?

  • Coherence & Cohesion স্কোর সরাসরি ওঠে
  • Examiner সহজে paragraph flow বোঝেন
  • TOEFL Independent / PTE Essay-তেও একই skill কাজে লাগে

যোগ করা (Addition)

LinkerUseবাংলাExample
moreoverin addition; used to add a stronger pointঅধিকন্তু / তাছাড়াওMoreover, online classes save travel time.
thirdlyused to introduce the third pointতৃতীয়তThirdly, citizens should follow traffic rules.
furthermorein addition; alsoঅধিকন্তু / আরও বলা যায়Furthermore, public transport reduces pollution.
secondlyused to introduce the second pointদ্বিতীয়তSecondly, roads need better maintenance.
in additionas well as what was saidএছাড়াওIn addition, free clinics help poor families.
firstlyused to introduce the first pointপ্রথমতFirstly, public transport must be affordable.
additionallyas an extra pointঅতিরিক্তভাবে / সেই সঙ্গেAdditionally, libraries offer free internet access.
finallyused to introduce the last pointসবশেষেFinally, governments should invest in education.

বিপরীত / Contrast

LinkerUseবাংলাExample
nonethelessin spite of what has just been saidতা সত্ত্বেও / তবুওIt rains often; nonetheless, tourism is strong.
on the other handused to present the opposite viewঅন্যদিকেOn the other hand, private schools may be expensive.
converselyin the opposite wayউল্টোদিকে / বিপরীতেCity life is busy; conversely, villages are quieter.
that saiddespite what has just been saidতা বলেও / তবে এ কথাও ঠিকOnline learning is useful; that said, labs still matter.
howeverbut; used to introduce contrastতবে / যাই হোকThe plan looks good; however, funding is limited.
despitewithout being affected byসত্ত্বেওDespite the cost, many people study abroad.
insteadas an alternativeপরিবর্তেInstead of memorising, practise with examples.
by contrastused when comparing opposite thingsতুলনায় / বিপরীতেBy contrast, older adults prefer face-to-face care.
whereasused to contrast two factsযেখানে / অন্যদিকেSome prefer cities, whereas others like villages.
althougheven thoughযদিওAlthough online learning is flexible, it needs discipline.
in contrastused to show a clear differenceবিপরীতেIn contrast, rural schools often lack labs.
neverthelessin spite of that; howeverতবুও / তারপরওThe course is difficult; nevertheless, many finish it.

ফলাফল (Result)

LinkerUseবাংলাExample
hencefor this reasonএজন্য / অতএবDemand fell; hence prices dropped.
otherwiseif not; or elseনা হলে / অন্যথায়Submit on time; otherwise you may lose marks.
thereforefor that reasonঅতএব / তাইTraffic increased; therefore commute times rose.
consequentlyas a resultফলস্বরূপ / এর ফলেFactories closed; consequently, unemployment rose.
accordinglyin a way that is suitable; thereforeসেই অনুযায়ী / তদনুসারেRules changed; schools acted accordingly.
thusin this way; thereforeএভাবে / অতএবShe practised daily; thus her band score improved.
as a resultbecause of somethingএর ফলেAs a result, more students chose online courses.

উদাহরণ

LinkerUseবাংলাExample
for exampleused to introduce an exampleউদাহরণস্বরূপFor example, cycling reduces carbon emissions.
particularlyespeciallyবিশেষ করেAir pollution is particularly severe in winter.
specificallyin a detailed or exact wayবিশেষভাবে / স্পষ্ট করেSpecifically, Task 2 needs a clear opinion.
for instanceas an exampleযেমন / উদাহরণ হিসেবেFor instance, Singapore taxes cars heavily.
notablyespecially; in a way worth mentioningউল্লেখযোগ্যভাবেNotably, women joined the workforce in large numbers.

তুলনা

LinkerUseবাংলাExample
likewisein the same way; alsoতেমনি / একইভাবেTeachers need training; likewise, parents need guidance.
similarlyin a similar wayএকইভাবেSimilarly, recycled paper can protect forests.
overallin general; considering everythingমোটের উপর / সামগ্রিকভাবেOverall, the advantages outweigh the drawbacks.
meanwhileat the same timeএই সময়ে / ইতোমধ্যেParents work; meanwhile, children attend school.

উপসংহার

LinkerUseবাংলাExample
in conclusionused to end an essayপরিশেষে / উপসংহারেIn conclusion, balanced policies are needed.
to summariseto give the main points briefly (UK)সংক্ষেপে বলতে গেলেTo summarise, education and health should come first.
to summarizeto give the main points briefly (US)সংক্ষেপে বলতে গেলেTo summarize, both sides have valid points.
in summarybriefly stating the main pointsসারকথায়In summary, investment in skills creates jobs.
Common mistake (BN learners)
Moreover sentence-এর মাঝে জোর করে ঢোকাবেন না। সাধারণত নতুন sentence বা semicolon-এর পরে শুরু হয়। Although-এর সাথে but একসাথে ব্যবহার করবেন না।
