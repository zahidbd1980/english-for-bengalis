Reading ও Writing দুটোতেই paraphrase দরকার। নিচে 30টি high-value word — বাংলা অর্থ + exam synonym mindset (unofficial)।

Trick
important → crucial/significant · show → indicate/illustrate · people → citizens/individuals · because → due to/since
WordবাংলাExample
importantগুরুত্বপূর্ণEducation is important for development.
crucialঅত্যন্ত গুরুত্বপূর্ণClean water is crucial for public health.
essentialঅপরিহার্যSleep is essential for memory.
showদেখানোThe chart shows a clear rise.
improveউন্নত করাPractice helps improve fluency.
enhanceউন্নীত করা / বাড়ানোTraining can enhance workplace skills.
reduceকমানোCycling can reduce traffic congestion.
increaseবাড়ানো / বৃদ্ধিCities need to increase green space.
problemসমস্যাAir pollution is a serious problem.
challengeচ্যালেঞ্জ / কঠিন কাজFinding affordable housing is a challenge.
advantageসুবিধাOne advantage of buses is low cost.
disadvantageঅসুবিধাA disadvantage of cars is pollution.
drawbackঅসুবিধা / নেতিবাচক দিকThe main drawback is high tuition fees.
peopleমানুষMany people work from home now.
citizenনাগরিকCitizens expect better public services.
helpসাহায্য করাMentors help new students settle in.
assistসহায়তা করাVolunteers assist elderly patients.
causeকারণ হওয়া / ঘটানোHeavy rain can cause floods.
becauseকারণI stayed home because I was ill.
due toএর কারণেClasses were cancelled due to the storm.
manyঅনেকMany students prefer online classes.
numerousঅসংখ্য / প্রচুরThere are numerous reasons for migration.
getপাওয়াStudents get feedback after each test.
obtainঅর্জন করা / পাওয়াApplicants must obtain a visa first.
thinkভাবা / মনে করাSome people think cities are safer.
believeবিশ্বাস করাI believe practice builds confidence.
goodভালোA good argument needs evidence.
effectiveকার্যকরClear feedback is effective for learners.
badখারাপBad diet affects concentration.
harmfulক্ষতিকরSmog is harmful to children.
