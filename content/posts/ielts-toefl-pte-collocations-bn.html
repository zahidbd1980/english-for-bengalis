Exam writing-এ collocation (শব্দের স্বাভাবিক জোড়া) ভুল হলে band/score কাটে — যেমন do a decision ❌, সঠিক make a decision ✅। নিচে 40টি exam-friendly collocation (unofficial)।

Memory tip
make → decision/effort/progress/mistake · do → research/homework/business · take → part/responsibility/advantage
WordবাংলাExample
make progressঅগ্রগতি করাRegular feedback helps students make progress.
make a decisionসিদ্ধান্ত নেওয়াGovernments must make a decision about funding.
make an effortচেষ্টা করাLearners should make an effort to speak daily.
do researchগবেষণা করাScientists do research on climate change.
take into accountবিবেচনায় নেওয়াPlanners must take into account local needs.
take part inঅংশ নেওয়াMany adults take part in evening classes.
pay attentionমনোযোগ দেওয়াPay attention to keywords in Listening.
play a roleভূমিকা রাখাTeachers play a role in student motivation.
have an impact onপ্রভাব ফেলাSocial media has an impact on teenagers.
raise awarenessসচেতনতা বাড়ানোCampaigns raise awareness about recycling.
meet the needsচাহিদা পূরণ করাPublic hospitals must meet the needs of all citizens.
provide access toপ্রবেশাধিকার দেওয়াLibraries provide access to reliable information.
pose a threatহুমকি সৃষ্টি করাPlastic waste poses a threat to oceans.
draw a conclusionসিদ্ধান্তে আসাReaders should draw a conclusion from the evidence.
reach a consensusঐকমত্যে পৌঁছানোExperts rarely reach a consensus quickly.
address the issueসমস্যা মোকাবিলা করাPolicies must address the issue of housing.
highly likelyঅত্যন্ত সম্ভাব্যIt is highly likely that demand will grow.
widely believedব্যাপকভাবে বিশ্বাস করা হয়It is widely believed that exercise improves mood.
key factorমূল কারণ / গুরুত্বপূর্ণ উপাদানCost is a key factor in choosing a university.
growing concernবাড়তে থাকা উদ্বেগThere is growing concern about screen time.
do homeworkবাড়ির কাজ করাChildren should do homework every day.
do businessব্যবসা করাMany firms do business online.
make a mistakeভুল করাIt is normal to make a mistake while learning.
make senseযুক্তিসংগত হওয়া / বোঝা যায়Your argument makes sense.
take responsibilityদায়িত্ব নেওয়াLeaders must take responsibility for results.
take advantage ofসুযোগ কাজে লাগানোStudents should take advantage of free libraries.
have difficultyঅসুবিধা হওয়াMany learners have difficulty with articles.
have access toব্যবহারের সুযোগ থাকাNot all villages have access to broadband.
give priority toঅগ্রাধিকার দেওয়াGovernments should give priority to health.
put pressure onচাপ দেওয়াParents may put pressure on children to score high.
come to an agreementএকমত হওয়াThe two sides came to an agreement.
keep in mindমনে রাখাKeep in mind the word limit.
lead toফলাফল হিসেবে ঘটানোPoor diet can lead to health problems.
result inপরিণতিতে ঘটাHeavy rain resulted in floods.
depend onনির্ভর করাSuccess depends on consistent practice.
focus onমনোযোগ দেওয়াFocus on Task 2 first.
based onভিত্তি করেThe essay is based on recent research.
in favour ofপক্ষেMany voters are in favour of reform.
in favor ofপক্ষেThe committee voted in favor of the plan.
on averageগড়েOn average, students study two hours a day.
