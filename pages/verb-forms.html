Gerund vs Participle — মনে রাখুন

Gerund (-ing → noun হিসেবে)
  • Subject হিসেবে: Reading is fun.
  • Object হিসেবে: I enjoy reading.
  • Preposition-এর পরে: good at writing
  • Verbs: enjoy / avoid / keep / finish / mind + gerund
Participle (-ing / -ed → adjective / clause)
  • Present participle: the running water
  • Past participle: a broken window
  • Participial clause: Feeling tired, she sat down.
  • Passive: It was written by her.
