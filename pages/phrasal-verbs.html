Separable vs Inseparable — মনে রাখুন

Separable (object মাঝে বসতে পারে)
  • Turn off the light. / Turn the light off.
  • Pronoun অবশ্যই মাঝে: Turn it off. (not *turn off it)
  • Common: put off, set up, fill in, give up
Inseparable / Intransitive
  • Inseparable: particle ভার্বের পাশেই — look after her
  • Intransitive: object নেই — The plane took off.
  • Common: look after, deal with, come across, get by
