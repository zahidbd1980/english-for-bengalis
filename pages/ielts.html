Four skills · চার স্কিল

  • Listening (~30 min + transfer): conversations, monologues, form/note completion — spelling must be exact.
  • Reading (60 min): Academic = 3 long passages; General Training = mixed texts. Skim, scan, then detail.
  • Writing (60 min): Task 1 (describe visual / letter) + Task 2 (essay). Task 2 carries more weight.
  • Speaking (~11–14 min): Part 1 short answers → Part 2 long turn → Part 3 discussion.

সময় ও ফরম্যাট পরিবর্তন হতে পারে — সর্বদা অফিসিয়াল সাইট চেক করুন।

Writing Task 2 skeleton

  1. Introduction: paraphrase the question + clear position.
  2. Body 1: one main idea + example.
  3. Body 2: second idea or opposite view + example.
  4. Conclusion: summarise your position (no new ideas).

Aim: clear paragraphs, topic sentences, and linking words (however, therefore, for example).

High-value academic words (starter)

analysis — বিশ্লেষণ
The analysis shows a clear trend.

significant — উল্লেখযোগ্য
There was a significant increase.

evidence — প্রমাণ
We need evidence to support the claim.

approach — পদ্ধতি / দৃষ্টিভঙ্গি
A new approach may help students.

benefit — উপকার
Online study has several benefits.

environment — পরিবেশ
Protecting the environment is important.

Listening spelling tip

In Listening form-completion, answers are marked wrong for spelling. Practise British/exam-style spellings carefully.

উদাহরণ: necessary, environment, accommodation, government, forty (not fourty).
